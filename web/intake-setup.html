READINESS

Your intake status

    Only configure this Microsoft login once, under Buyer. Leave Seller empty when using a single account. Log in from Controls & Console first.

    01 / PAYOUTS

    Set automatic seller payments

    Choose who may receive LTC and how much the bot may pay. Your wallet remains subject to these limits and its available confirmed funds.

    02 / MONEY DETECTION

    Capture the server’s incoming receipt

    Pause intake, start a capture, and have another player send a small known test amount to your Buyer account. The bot only records replies here; it does not send coins or LTC.

    Select the actual incoming payment line, not the /bal reply.

    Every real sale checks the sender, the requested amount, and an exact before/after balance increase. Rounded balances such as “1.2M” cannot establish an exact delta; the bot will stop and explain this if the server does not provide a precise balance.

    03 / BEFORE ACCEPTING CUSTOMERS

    Finish with one small sale

    Resume intake and test Sell Money with a known amount. Wait for the bot’s explicit payment instruction before sending. A transfer made outside an armed sale is not paid automatically.

    Spawner collection also needs your working home and Ender Chest. Money intake does not use home/storage to verify a receipt.
